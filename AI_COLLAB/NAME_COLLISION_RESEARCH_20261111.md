## 2. Collisions found for the current name "PocketCode"
- **Pocket Code by Catrobat** (a visual programming app for phones, open source under AGPL, run by a nonprofit association; on Google Play with about 4.3 million installs reported, and on the iOS App Store as "Pocket Code", bundle org.catrobat.pocketcode; first released 2013) (S: two app-analytics pages, the Catrobat GitHub wiki (V page), and an academic paper).
- This is the same market (programming on a phone) and the same name apart from a space. The repository's current name is therefore also at risk of confusion and of being overshadowed in search.

## 3. Conclusion (PROPOSED)
Both names have real collisions in the market PoCo cares about. "PoCo" carries the more serious legal risk (a registered trademark of a large company in the software class); "PocketCode" carries a confusion risk with an established, non-profit coding app. Do not rename the repository, the documents or the website yet, and do not publish the name PoCo anywhere. Treat both names as provisional until a clearance step is done.

## 4. Free clearance checklist for any candidate name
1. Search the App Store on the iPhone and Google Play for the name and close variants (no spend).
2. Search GitHub (repositories, organisations and users) and the main search engines.
3. Check domain availability with a free lookup, and the main social handles.
4. Search trademark databases that are free: the USPTO trademark search, and, depending on where the owner lives and sells, the Japanese J-PlatPat and EUIPO TMview.
5. Prefer a distinctive coined or compound name over a common word; avoid names already used in software classes 9 and 42.
6. Record the result with dates and URLs here; only then decide.
