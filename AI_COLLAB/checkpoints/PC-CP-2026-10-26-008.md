## Work completed

Static inspection found that `bool(result.get("stdout_truncated", False))` and its stderr equivalent treat non-empty strings such as `"false"` as true. That could produce a false truncation disclosure when a caller supplies malformed metadata.

Changed `scripts/report_contract.py` to accept a source truncation flag only when it is the boolean `True`. A non-boolean flag is ignored and disclosed in uncertainty notes. Actual formatter truncation still independently sets the truncation indicator.

Added `test_string_false_truncation_flag_is_not_treated_as_true` to `scripts/test_report_contract.py`, covering a string-valued `"false"` flag, the resulting non-truncated status, and the malformed-flag diagnostic.
